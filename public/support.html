Common questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone?

Open Keeper and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Keeper. Apple has step-by-step instructions.

Keeper only shows some of my photos

You probably gave Keeper limited access. Keeper can only see the photos you allowed. To change it, open Settings, tap Apps, then Keeper, then Photos, and choose Full Access.

I deleted a photo by mistake

Deleted photos go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app for 30 days. Here is how to recover them.

Why does iOS ask me to confirm deleting?

Keeper deletes through Apple's Photos framework, and iOS always asks before photos are removed from your library. You confirm once for the whole session when you tap Save.
