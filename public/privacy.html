Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Keeper is a photo library review app. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it — the short version is: your photos never leave your device.

Photo library access

Keeper requests access to your photo library to display photos for review and to delete the ones you choose. This happens entirely through Apple's PhotosKit framework, on your device. Keeper does not upload, transmit, or store copies of your photos anywhere — not to us, not to any third party.

Location and camera metadata

When a photo has location data attached, Keeper reads the coordinates already embedded in that photo and asks Apple's geocoding service to resolve them into a readable place name (e.g. "Sydney, Australia") for display. This request goes directly from your device to Apple — Keeper does not operate this service, see the result separately, or store it. Camera model information is read from the photo's own metadata and never leaves your device.

Purchases

Keeper Pro subscriptions are paid through Apple's App Store. Apple handles billing, and Keeper never sees or stores your payment details.

Subscription screens (Superwall)

Keeper uses Superwall to show its subscription screens and to see how they perform. To do this, the app sends Superwall:

  • Your purchase and subscription status, and which subscription screens you viewed.
  • Your answers to the setup questions (such as your goals and how long you want to spend each day), and the plan Keeper made from them.
  • Counts from your library, such as how many photos, videos and screenshots it has and the year of the oldest photo. No photos, file names, locations or other photo details are sent.
  • Basic device information, such as the device model, iOS version, language, region and an identifier Apple provides to apps from the same developer.

This data is not linked to your name, email or any account, and it is not used for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.
  • No advertising SDKs, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't collect your name, email, or contact details unless you choose to email us directly for support.

Data stored on your device

Keeper keeps a small local record of which photos you've decided to keep (so it can show you your review history) and some usage statistics (like your review streak). This stays on your device. You can clear it at any time from Settings → Reset Progress.

Children's privacy

Keeper is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will reflect it. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the update.
