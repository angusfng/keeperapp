Optimize iPhone Storage: what it does and how to turn it on or off

What the setting actually keeps on your phone, how it compares with Download and Keep Originals, and how to switch between them.

Updated November 24, 2026

Short answer: Optimize iPhone Storage is an iCloud Photos setting. With it on, your full-resolution photos and videos are stored in iCloud and your iPhone keeps space-saving versions. It's on by default when you use iCloud Photos. To turn it off, choose Download and Keep Originals in the same settings.

What does Optimize iPhone Storage mean?

It means your iPhone doesn't have to hold the full-size copy of every photo. With the setting on, iCloud Photos stores your photos and videos in their original, high-resolution version, and your device keeps space-saving versions.

When your iPhone needs more room, iCloud Photos optimizes your library, starting with the photos and videos you access least. You can download originals over Wi-Fi or cellular.

The setting comes with iCloud Photos, and it's on by default when you use iCloud Photos. Without it, your photos and videos are stored on your device in their original, high-resolution version, which uses a lot of space.

Optimize iPhone Storage or Download and Keep Originals?

If your phone is full, Optimize iPhone Storage is the setting that saves space. Download and Keep Originals is the other choice on the same screen. Apple's iPhone User Guide lists it as the way to turn off Optimize Storage. Apple also has you choose it before you copy photos from your iPhone to an external drive.

Originals take up a lot of room, so check Settings > General > iPhone Storage before you switch. That screen lists your apps and the space each one uses. To get a rough idea of how much space a library of your size takes up, try the photo storage calculator.

How to turn Optimize iPhone Storage on or off

Both choices are in your iCloud Photos settings, and the steps changed in iOS 27. Apple's pages use both names, Optimize Storage and Optimize iPhone Storage, for the same setting. Start at Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Photos, then:

Steps to turn Optimize iPhone Storage on or off by iOS version
To do thisiOS 27 or lateriOS 26 or earlier
Turn it onTap iPhone Storage, then make sure Optimize iPhone Storage is onChoose Optimize Storage
Turn it offTap iPhone Storage, then tap Download and Keep OriginalsTap Download and Keep Originals

Both choices are part of iCloud Photos, which you turn on with Sync this iPhone on the same Photos screen.

On a Mac, the matching setting is in the Photos app. Choose Photos > Settings (or Preferences), click the iCloud tab, then pick a storage setting. Download Originals to this Mac keeps the full-size files in iCloud and on your Mac.

Why isn't Optimize iPhone Storage freeing up space?

iCloud Photos optimizes your library when you need more device storage. If space is still tight, look at two other things.

One is iCloud itself. Your originals live there, and you can store as many photos and videos as you want as long as you have enough space in iCloud. If you run out, new photos and videos won't upload to iCloud Photos. When you set up iCloud, you get 5 GB of storage for free. To see what you're using, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud, then tap Storage.

The other is whether Photos is really what's filling the phone. The list in Settings > General > iPhone Storage shows each app's share. In iOS 17 or later, Recommended for You can show photos, large files or backups that you might not need anymore and can delete. Merging duplicate photos and videos can also free up space on your device (see how to delete duplicate photos on iPhone).

When the library itself is too big

Optimize iPhone Storage helps the phone, but your originals still use your iCloud storage. If both are full, you can buy more iCloud storage with iCloud+, or make the library smaller. Start with the obvious ones, like ten shots of the same sunset or screenshots you no longer need.

Before you delete, know how iCloud Photos handles it. Photos you delete on one device are deleted everywhere you use iCloud Photos. They stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, and after that they're gone for good. If you're over your iCloud storage limit, Apple says deleted photos are removed immediately and can't be recovered from Recently Deleted. Your photos also aren't duplicated in your iCloud backup, so keep a copy of anything you can't replace. For photos you want to keep but don't need in iCloud, you can transfer them to your computer. If you want photos off your phone but still in iCloud, see delete from iPhone but not iCloud.

You can sort through a library in the Photos app with Select mode, which our guide to deleting photos on iPhone walks through. Another option is Keeper, which shows your photos one at a time, full screen, so you can swipe right to keep and left to delete. You can start with Oldest First or work through one month at a time. Nothing is deleted until you tap Save, and afterward it shows how much storage you freed.

Common questions

Does Optimize iPhone Storage delete my photos?

No. Apple says your full-resolution originals are stored in iCloud, and your iPhone keeps space-saving versions. You can download an original over Wi-Fi or cellular.

Is Optimize iPhone Storage on by default?

Yes. Optimize iPhone Storage is turned on by default when you use iCloud Photos, but you can turn it off.

Does Optimize iPhone Storage free up iCloud storage?

No. Apple describes it as a way to save space on your device. Your full-resolution originals are still stored in iCloud, so they still use your iCloud storage.

Is my iCloud backup a copy of my photos?

Not when you use iCloud Photos. Your photos aren't duplicated in your iCloud backup, so Apple says to keep backup copies of your library.
