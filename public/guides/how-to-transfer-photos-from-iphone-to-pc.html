How to transfer photos from iPhone to a PC

You can copy iPhone photos to a Windows PC over a USB cable, or download them from iCloud without one. Here are both ways, and what to check before you delete anything from your phone.

Updated November 26, 2026

Short answer: To transfer photos from iPhone to a PC, install the Apple Devices app from the Microsoft Store and connect your iPhone with a USB cable. With the iPhone unlocked, tap Trust or Allow, then open the Photos app on your PC, select Import and pick your iPhone. If you use iCloud Photos, download the full-size originals to your iPhone first, or download them from iCloud.com instead.

How to transfer photos from iPhone to PC with a cable

A USB cable and the Windows Photos app do the job. Apple and Microsoft each cover half of the steps, so here they are together:

  1. On your PC, install the Apple Devices app from the Microsoft Store.
  2. Connect your iPhone to the PC with a USB cable that can transfer files.
  3. Keep the iPhone unlocked. Microsoft says your PC can't find the device while it's locked.
  4. If your iPhone asks whether to trust this computer, tap Trust or Allow.
  5. On the PC, select the Start button, then Photos.
  6. Select Import, then select your iPhone and follow the instructions.
  7. Choose the photos and videos you want and where to save them, then wait for the transfer to finish.

If the PC doesn't see your iPhone, check that it's unlocked and that you tapped Trust. Microsoft also suggests trying a different USB cable.

If you use iCloud Photos, get the originals first

Apple says that with iCloud Photos turned on, you need to download the original, full-resolution versions to your iPhone before you import them to your PC. Optimize iPhone Storage, which is on by default, keeps the full-resolution originals in iCloud and smaller versions on your phone. Microsoft warns that you may not be able to import photos that are in iCloud but not on the device.

To keep the originals on your iPhone, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Photos, then:

Steps to download originals to iPhone by iOS version

iOS 27 or lateriPhone Storage, then Download and Keep Originals

If your iPhone doesn't have room for the originals, download them from iCloud.com instead and choose Unmodified Originals under More Download Options.

How to transfer photos from iPhone to PC wirelessly

If your photos are in iCloud Photos, you can skip the cable and download them in a web browser:

  1. On your PC, go to iCloud.com/photos and sign in to your Apple Account.
  2. Select the photos and videos you want. Hold the Control key to pick more than one. You can select up to 1,000 at a time.
  3. Click the Download button in the upper-right corner. To choose a format first, click the More button, choose More Download Options, pick a format, then click Download.

Apple lists three formats there. Unmodified Originals keeps the format the photo was taken or imported in. Highest Resolution is typically HEIC or H.265. Most Compatible gives you JPEG or MP4/H.264.

Save photos in a format Windows can open

For the best compatibility with Windows, Apple suggests changing the camera setting. Go to Settings > Camera > Formats and tap Most Compatible. From then on, your camera takes photos and videos in JPEG and H.264.

This only changes new photos. If you use iCloud Photos, you can get JPEG or MP4 copies of older ones by choosing Most Compatible when you download from iCloud.com.

Deleting photos from your iPhone after the transfer

If you moved photos to clear space on your phone, check them before you delete anything. Make sure the copies you're keeping came from the USB import or an iCloud.com download. Open a few of the copies on your PC, including a video, and make sure they play. Apple also says to back up the photos you still want before you delete.

If you use iCloud Photos, deleting a photo on your iPhone also deletes it on all your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. To free up phone space without deleting from iCloud, see how to delete photos from iPhone but not iCloud. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted, and Apple says you can recover them for 30 days.

You don't have to move everything, either. If your camera roll has 20 shots of the same sunset, deleting the extras first leaves less to move. The photo storage calculator gives a rough idea of the space they take. If you'd rather sort them one at a time, Keeper shows your photos full screen and you swipe right to keep or left to delete. You can start with your oldest photos or clear one month at a time, and nothing is deleted until you tap Save.

Common questions

Why can't my PC see my iPhone photos?

Microsoft says your PC can't find the iPhone if it's locked, so unlock it and tap Trust or Allow if your iPhone asks. If it still doesn't connect, Microsoft suggests trying a different USB cable. If iCloud Photos is on, some photos may be in iCloud but not on the phone, and you may not be able to import those over the cable.

How do I transfer photos from iPhone to PC wirelessly?

Use iCloud Photos. On iCloud.com/photos you can select up to 1,000 photos at a time and click Download.

How do I make iPhone photos open on Windows?

For the best compatibility with Windows, Apple suggests going to Settings, then Camera, then Formats, and choosing Most Compatible. From then on, your camera takes photos and videos in JPEG and H.264. When you download from iCloud.com, you can also choose Most Compatible under More Download Options.

Can I delete photos from my iPhone after moving them to a PC?

Yes, but check the copies on your PC first, and make sure they came from the USB import or an iCloud.com download. If you use iCloud Photos, deleting a photo on your iPhone also deletes it on your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Deleted photos can be recovered from Recently Deleted for 30 days.
