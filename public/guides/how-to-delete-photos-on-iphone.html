How to delete photos on iPhone

The steps in the Photos app for one photo or a whole batch, where deleted photos go, and why some photos can't be deleted.

Updated November 18, 2026

Short answer: Open the photo in the Photos app, tap the trash can, then tap Delete Photo. To delete several at once, tap Select, tap each photo you want gone (or drag your finger across a row), then tap the trash can. Deleted photos sit in Recently Deleted for 30 days before they're removed for good.

How to delete one photo on iPhone

This is the quickest way when you're already looking at the photo you want gone. Apple's steps are short:

  1. Open the Photos app and tap the photo or video.
  2. Tap the trash can button.
  3. Tap Delete Photo to confirm.

The photo leaves your library straight away and moves to Recently Deleted. Nothing else changes, so it's safe to try on a photo you're unsure about.

How to delete multiple photos at once

Use Select mode. In your library, tap Select, then tap each photo and video you want to remove. Apple notes you can also drag across several items to pick a run of them in one go, which is much faster than tapping a long row one by one. Then tap the trash can and confirm.

Select mode works well for a burst of near-identical shots or an afternoon of screenshots that sit together. It gets slow for a whole camera roll, because you have to judge dozens of small thumbnails at once and it's easy to tick a photo you meant to keep.

What happens after you delete a photo

Deleted photos and videos go to the Recently Deleted album and stay there for 30 days. During that time you can recover them or remove them for good. After 30 days they're gone.

If you use iCloud Photos, a delete isn't limited to your iPhone. Apple says a photo you delete is deleted on all your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account, so it disappears from your iPad and Mac too. There's one more catch: if you're over your iCloud storage limit, your device removes deleted photos immediately, and they can't be recovered from Recently Deleted. If you want photos off your phone but still in iCloud, read deleting photos from iPhone but not iCloud first.

Why can't I delete some photos on my iPhone?

If the trash can is gray, the photos were probably synced to your iPhone from a computer. Apple says you can't delete those in the Photos app on the phone itself. Stop syncing those photos from your computer, and they'll come off the iPhone.

How to clear a big camera roll faster

Start with exact copies. Since iOS 16, the Photos app finds duplicate photos and puts them in a Duplicates album under Utilities in the Collections tab. Open it and tap Merge on each set. Detection only runs while the iPhone is locked and charging, so on a big library the album can take a few days to appear.

The rest needs a person to decide, one photo at a time: the blurry shot next to the good one, the ten tries at the same sunset, the parking spot you photographed in 2021. Keeper is built for that part. It shows your photos full screen, one at a time, and you swipe right to keep or left to delete. Nothing is deleted while you swipe. Your choices wait until you tap Save, then iOS asks you to confirm once, and the deleted photos go to Recently Deleted like any other. You can start from your oldest photos, a single album or today's date in earlier years, and stop whenever you like.

Common questions

Can I get deleted photos back on iPhone?

Yes, for 30 days. Open Photos, tap Collections, then Recently Deleted under Utilities. Unlock it, tap Select, choose the photos, tap the More button and tap Recover. The exception is when you're over your iCloud storage limit, because deleted photos are then removed straight away.

Does deleting a photo on iPhone delete it from iCloud?

If iCloud Photos is on, yes. Apple says a deleted photo is deleted on every device signed in with the same Apple Account. It stays recoverable in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

How do I permanently delete photos on iPhone?

Delete them as usual, then open the Recently Deleted album, unlock it, tap Select, choose the photos, tap the More button and tap Delete. Apple says you can't get a photo back after you delete it from Recently Deleted.

Why is the trash can gray on some photos?

Those photos were most likely synced to your iPhone from a computer. Apple says they can't be deleted in the Photos app on the phone. Stop syncing them from the computer instead.
