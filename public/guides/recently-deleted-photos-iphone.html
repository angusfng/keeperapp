Recently Deleted on iPhone: recover photos or delete them for good

Where the album lives, the steps to bring photos back or clear them out, and the cases where a deleted photo never reaches it.

Updated November 18, 2026

Short answer: Recently Deleted is in the Photos app under Collections, in the Utilities section. Deleted photos and videos stay there for 30 days. Open it, unlock it with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, tap Select, pick the photos, then tap the More button and choose Recover or Delete.

Where is Recently Deleted on iPhone?

Open the Photos app and tap Collections. Scroll to the Utilities section and tap Recently Deleted. The album is locked by default, so tap View Album and unlock it with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

Every photo or video you delete lands here first, whether you deleted it in the Photos app or in an app like Keeper that uses your photo library. It stays for 30 days, then it's removed for good.

How to recover recently deleted photos

  1. Open Recently Deleted and unlock it.
  2. Tap Select, then tap the photos and videos you want back.
  3. Tap the More button (the three dots), then tap Recover. To bring back everything, choose Recover All.

Recovered photos go back to your library. If you use iCloud Photos, they come back on your other devices too.

How to permanently delete photos from Recently Deleted

Follow the same steps, but tap Delete (or Delete All) instead of Recover, then confirm. Apple is clear that this is final: once you delete a photo from this album, you can't get it back. Do it when you're sure, for example after you've checked a big batch of deletions and want the album empty.

In a Shared Library, anyone can delete a photo, but only the person who added it can recover or permanently delete it. That's why the confirm button may say Delete Photos I Added.

Why does Recently Deleted ask for Face ID?

Because the album holds photos you chose to remove, Apple locks it, along with the Hidden album. If you'd rather open it without unlocking, go to Settings > Apps > Photos and turn off Use Face ID (or Use Touch ID or Use Passcode, depending on your iPhone). Apple's guide covers this setting.

When deleted photos skip Recently Deleted

There's one case where the 30-day safety net doesn't apply. If you've gone over your iCloud storage limit, your device immediately removes any photos and videos you delete, and you can't recover them from Recently Deleted. Check your storage in Settings > [your name] > iCloud before a big cleanup if you're close to full.

Photos waiting in Recently Deleted don't count toward your iCloud storage, so you don't need to empty it just to get iCloud space back.

Using Recently Deleted as a safety net for a cleanup

The 30 days make a big cleanup a lot less stressful. Delete with confidence, then scroll through Recently Deleted once at the end of the session to catch anything you regret. Keeper works with this: you swipe through photos one at a time, your choices are only applied when you tap Save, and everything you delete goes into Recently Deleted like a normal delete. Common questions

How long do photos stay in Recently Deleted?

30 days. During that time you can recover them or delete them for good. After 30 days they're removed automatically.

Do photos in Recently Deleted use iCloud storage?

No. Apple says items in Recently Deleted don't count toward your iCloud storage.

Can I recover photos after deleting them from Recently Deleted?

No. Apple says that when you delete a photo or video from the Recently Deleted album, you can't get it back.

Why are my deleted photos not in Recently Deleted?

If you were over your iCloud storage limit when you deleted them, your device removed them immediately and they can't be recovered from Recently Deleted. Photos deleted more than 30 days ago are also gone.
