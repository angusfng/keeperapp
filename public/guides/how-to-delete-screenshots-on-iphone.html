How to delete screenshots on iPhone

Your iPhone keeps every screenshot in one place in the Photos app, which makes them easy to clear out in a batch. Here's how to find them, delete them, and get one back if you remove too many.

Updated November 21, 2026

Short answer: To delete screenshots on iPhone, open Photos, tap Collections, scroll down to Media Types and tap Screenshots. Tap Select, slide your finger across the screenshots you want gone, then tap the Trash button and confirm. They usually stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, unless you're over your iCloud storage limit, when they're removed right away.

How to delete screenshots on iPhone

Start in the Screenshots collection, because it holds nothing else. Apple says screenshots are saved to your photo library automatically, and the Photos app sorts them into their own group. That means you won't scroll past your vacation photos to find them.

  1. Open the Photos app and tap Collections.
  2. Scroll down to Media Types, then tap Screenshots.
  3. Tap Select, then tap each screenshot you want to delete.
  4. Tap the Trash button, then confirm.

Apple gives this Collections, Media Types, Screenshots path on its screenshot support page. In iOS 26, Screenshots is also one of the default Pinned Collections, along with Favorites, Recently Saved, Map and Videos. If it's missing from yours, tap Collections, tap Pinned, then tap Edit to add it back.

How to delete a lot of screenshots at once

Slide your finger instead of tapping. After you tap Select, Apple says you can slide your finger across multiple photos to select more than one. One drag can pick up a run of screenshots, instead of one tap for each. Then tap the Trash button and confirm.

Before you clear years of them, take a slow look. Screenshots are often the only copy of something: a parking spot, a confirmation number, a recipe from a site that no longer exists, or a text from someone you can't ask again. Pull out anything you still need before you select the rest.

To check your storage, go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage. The photo storage calculator gives a rough idea of what a cleanup would free.

Where deleted screenshots go

Deleted screenshots go to the Recently Deleted album, the same as any photo. Apple says items stay there for 30 days, and after that they're permanently deleted. During those 30 days you can bring one back:

  1. Open Photos and tap Collections.
  2. Swipe up, then tap Recently Deleted under Utilities.
  3. Tap View Album and unlock it with Face ID or Touch ID (iOS 16 or later).
  4. Tap Select, pick the screenshots, tap the More button, then tap Recover.

To remove them sooner, follow the same steps but tap Delete or Delete All instead, then confirm. Apple says you can't get a photo back after you delete it from this album.

Two more things to know. If you use iCloud Photos, a screenshot you delete is deleted on all your devices. To keep a copy in iCloud, see deleting from iPhone but not iCloud. And if you're over your iCloud storage limit, Apple says deleted items are removed immediately and can't be recovered from Recently Deleted.

Check for duplicates

The Photos app also has a Duplicates collection for duplicate photos and videos in your library. Open Photos, tap Collections, tap Utilities, then tap Duplicates. Apple says you can tap Merge to combine a set, then tap Merge [number] Items to confirm. The collection only appears when your library has duplicates. To merge them all at once, see how to delete duplicate photos on iPhone.

Going through screenshots one at a time

Bulk delete is fast, but it's easy to sweep away something you meant to keep. If your screenshots are mixed in with things you still need, look at each one at full size instead of as a small thumbnail.

Keeper shows the photos in your library one at a time, full screen, so you can swipe right to keep and left to delete. It doesn't pick out screenshots for you, but nothing is deleted until you tap Save, and deleted items go to Recently Deleted like any other.

Common questions

Can I get deleted screenshots back?

Yes, for 30 days. Open Photos, tap Collections, then tap Recently Deleted under Utilities. Tap View Album and unlock it with Face ID or Touch ID. Tap Select, pick the screenshots, tap the More button, then tap Recover. If you're over your iCloud storage limit, deleted items are removed right away and can't be recovered.

Does deleting screenshots on iPhone delete them from iCloud?

If you use iCloud Photos, yes. Apple says a photo you delete on one device is deleted on all your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account.

Where are screenshots saved on iPhone?

Apple says screenshots are saved to your photo library automatically. Open Photos, tap Collections, scroll down to Media Types, then tap Screenshots to see them all in one place.
