How to delete photos from iPhone but not iCloud

Why the obvious approach doesn't work, and two ways to get space back on your phone while keeping your photos.

Updated November 18, 2026

Short answer: With iCloud Photos on, you can't. Apple says photos you delete on one device are deleted everywhere you use iCloud Photos. To free space on your iPhone and keep your photos in iCloud, turn on Optimize iPhone Storage instead. To keep a copy outside iCloud, transfer the photos to a computer before you delete them.

Why deleting a photo on iPhone deletes it from iCloud

iCloud Photos keeps one library in sync across your devices. That's what makes a photo taken on your iPhone show up on your Mac, and it works for deletes too. Apple's wording is direct: when you delete photos and videos on one device, they're deleted everywhere that you use iCloud Photos.

The delete isn't instant and final. The photo goes to Recently Deleted, where you can recover it for 30 days. So if you've already deleted photos hoping they'd stay in iCloud, check that album now.

Option 1: free up space with Optimize iPhone Storage

Most people searching this want their phone's storage back, not their photos gone. That's what Optimize iPhone Storage is for. With it on, your device keeps space-saving versions of your photos and videos, and iCloud keeps the full-resolution originals.

  • iOS 27 or later: Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Photos, tap iPhone Storage, and turn on Optimize iPhone Storage.
  • iOS 26 or earlier: Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Photos, then choose Optimize Storage.

Every photo still shows in your library, and nothing is deleted anywhere.

Option 2: keep a copy outside iCloud first

If you want photos off your iPhone and out of iCloud, but not gone forever, make a copy somewhere else before you delete. Apple suggests you transfer photos to your computer when you don't want to store them in iCloud. It also says to back up the photos you still want before you delete anything.

One detail catches people out. Your photos aren't duplicated in your iCloud backup when you use iCloud Photos, so an iPhone backup isn't a second copy of your library. Make sure the copy on your computer or drive is a set of real files you can open, then delete.

What about turning off iCloud Photos?

The switch is in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Photos, labeled Sync this iPhone. Apple's iCloud Photos page doesn't spell out what turning it off does to the photos already on your phone, especially when Optimize is on and only smaller versions are stored locally. If you go this way, read the message iOS shows before you confirm, and make sure you have a full copy of your library somewhere first.

Common questions

If I delete a photo from my iPhone, will it delete from iCloud?

Yes, if iCloud Photos is on. Apple says photos you delete on one device are deleted everywhere you use iCloud Photos. You can recover them from Recently Deleted for 30 days.

How do I free up iPhone space without deleting photos from iCloud?

Turn on Optimize iPhone Storage in your iCloud Photos settings. Your iPhone keeps space-saving versions and iCloud keeps the full-resolution originals.

Is my iPhone backup a copy of my photos?

Not when you use iCloud Photos. Apple says photos in iCloud Photos aren't duplicated in your iCloud backup, so keep a separate copy of your library.

Can I get back photos I deleted by mistake?

Yes, within 30 days. Open Recently Deleted in the Photos app, unlock it, select the photos, tap the More button and tap Recover.
