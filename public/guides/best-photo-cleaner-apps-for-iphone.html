The best photo cleaner apps for iPhone, compared

Keeper is our app, so we've stuck to what each app's own App Store listing says. We start with the free tools already in your Photos app, then cover three apps, each for a different kind of cleanup.

Updated November 28, 2026

Short answer: The best photo cleaner app for iPhone depends on the job. For duplicates and screenshots, start with the free tools in the Photos app. CleanMy®Phone's listing says it scans for duplicates, blurry photos, old screenshots and large videos, while Slidebox and Keeper let you go through photos by hand with swipes.

The free option: clean up photos in the Photos app

The Photos app can already handle duplicates and screenshots, and it costs nothing. Start with duplicates. On iOS 26 and iOS 27, Apple's iPhone User Guide gives these steps to merge duplicates:

  1. Open the Photos app and tap Collections.
  2. Tap Utilities, then tap Duplicates.
  3. Tap Merge on a set, then tap Merge [number] Items to confirm.

To merge every set at once, Apple says to tap Select, then Select All, then Merge, then tap Merge [number] Items to confirm. Merge combines the highest image quality and relevant data into one photo, and the other copies move to Recently Deleted. The Duplicates album needs iOS 16 or later, and it only appears when your library has duplicates. If your Photos app looks different, our guide to deleting duplicate photos gives the path for each iOS version.

For screenshots, Apple says to open Photos, go to Collections and tap Media Types, then tap Screenshots. To delete a batch, tap Select, slide your finger across the ones you want gone, then tap the Trash button and confirm. They go to Recently Deleted for 30 days before they're permanently deleted. Our guides to deleting screenshots and deleting photos on iPhone have more detail.

To see what else takes up space, go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage. Apple also says that in iOS 17 or later, Recommended for You lets you check for photos, large files or backups you might not need anymore.

CleanMy®Phone: an app that finds the clutter for you

CleanMy®Phone, from MacPaw, fits best if you want an app to do the searching. Its App Store listing says it scans your whole library for blurred photos, old screenshots, large videos and duplicates. It also says it finds similar shots and helps you pick the best one to keep. The listing names a few other tools too: compressing videos, turning Live Photos into still images, merging similar contacts, and AI that sorts photos into groups like Travel, Pets, Food and Documents.

The listing says you decide which photos and videos it removes, and that you can recover an accidental deletion from Recently Deleted. It says the app works fully on-device, and you can mark photos as Sensitive so they're never included in cleanups. It's free to download with in-app purchases, and the listing describes a 1-year subscription with a three-day free trial. It needs iOS 17 or later.

Pick it if you have years of blurry shots and big videos and would rather not look at every photo yourself.

Slidebox, for sorting photos into albums as you delete

Slidebox suits people who want to file photos into albums while they clean. Its listing says you delete a photo with a swipe and sort it into an album with a single tap. It also says you can swipe between duplicate photos to decide which to keep, mark favorites and undo any action. The listing says it works directly with the Photos app, and that changes sync across your iPhone and iCloud.

Slidebox is free to download with in-app purchases. Its listing mentions monthly and annual subscriptions, plus a one-time purchase that unlocks the full version. It needs iOS 15 or later.

Slidebox makes most sense when your camera roll needs sorting as much as trimming, like a year of trip photos you'd like in their own album.

Keeper: one photo at a time, full screen

Keeper is for going through your library by hand, one decision at a time: the blurry shot next to the good one, or ten tries at the same sunset. Keeper shows your photos and videos one at a time, full screen. Swipe right to keep and left to delete, or use the keep and delete buttons. Nothing is deleted while you swipe. Your choices wait until you tap Save, then iOS asks you to confirm once, and deleted photos go to Recently Deleted like any other. You can start from Oldest First, Newest First, a random spot or one month at a time, or stick to a single album.

Keeper doesn't find duplicates, blurry photos or screenshots for you. If that's the job, the Duplicates album in Photos or CleanMy®Phone is a better fit. Keeper is free to download, but you need a weekly or yearly subscription to use it. It needs iOS 18 or later.

It's a good fit if you want to see every photo at full size before you decide, and stop and pick up later.

Which photo cleaner app should you use?

Pick by the job you need done:

  • If you want to clear duplicates for free, use the Duplicates album in Photos.
  • If you want an app to find blurry shots, old screenshots and large videos, CleanMy®Phone's listing says that's what it scans for.
  • If you want to sort photos into albums as you delete, that's what Slidebox's listing describes.
  • If you want to look at each photo yourself, Slidebox and Keeper both use swipes.

Whichever you choose, Apple suggests you back up the photos you still want before you delete. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then they're permanently deleted. To guess how much space a cleanup could free, try the photo storage calculator.

Common questions

Is there a free photo cleaner app for iPhone?

The Photos app is free and already on your iPhone. On iOS 16 or later it has a Duplicates album for merging duplicates. It also keeps screenshots in their own collection under Media Types. CleanMy®Phone and Slidebox are free to download with in-app purchases. Keeper is free to download, but you need a subscription to use it.

Do photo cleaner apps delete photos from iCloud too?

Apple says that when you use iCloud Photos and delete a photo on one device, it gets deleted on all your other devices. Keeper works the same way as the Photos app here. Slidebox's listing says its changes sync across your iPhone and iCloud. To clear space on your iPhone but keep photos in iCloud, see how to delete photos from iPhone but not iCloud.

Can I get back photos a cleaner app deleted?

Photos you delete in the Photos app go to Recently Deleted for 30 days. Keeper sends deleted photos there too, and CleanMy®Phone's listing says you can recover an accidental deletion from Recently Deleted. After 30 days they're permanently deleted, and Apple says that once you delete a photo from that album, you can't get it back.

Which photo cleaner app finds blurry photos?

CleanMy®Phone's listing says it scans your library for blurred photos. Slidebox's listing doesn't mention finding blurry photos, and Keeper doesn't find them for you.
