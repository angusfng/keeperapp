How to delete duplicate photos on iPhone

The Photos app finds duplicate photos and videos for you and lets you merge them in a few taps. Here's where to find them in your iOS version, what merging keeps, and how to undo it.

Updated November 21, 2026

Short answer: To delete duplicate photos on iPhone, open Photos and go to the Duplicates album under Utilities (on iOS 26 or later, tap Collections first). Tap Merge on a set, or tap Select, then Select All, then Merge to do them all, and confirm.

How to delete duplicate photos on iPhone

Use the Duplicates album in the Photos app. Photos looks for duplicate photos and videos on its own and puts them in one album. On iOS 26 and later, here's how Apple says to merge duplicates:

  1. Open the Photos app and tap Collections.
  2. Tap Utilities, then tap Duplicates.
  3. Find a set and tap Merge.
  4. Tap Merge [number] Items to confirm.

The Photos app has moved this album around between versions. If you don't see Collections, find your version below.

Where to find the Duplicates album in each iOS version
iOS versionWhere Duplicates is
iOS 26 or laterCollections, then Utilities, then Duplicates (iOS 26, iOS 27)
iOS 18Utilities, then Duplicates
iOS 17Albums tab, then Duplicates under Utilities
iOS 16Albums tab, then Duplicates below Utilities
iOS 15 or earlierNo Duplicates album. It started with iOS 16.

On iOS 16 and 17, Apple's guide says the copies in each set appear next to each other.

How to merge all duplicates at once

Tap Select, then Select All, before you tap Merge. Apple's delete photos page says to tap Select, then tap Select on each set you want or tap Select All. Then tap Merge and tap Merge [number] Items to confirm. Apple's page doesn't give an iOS version for Select All. If you don't see it, tap Merge on each set instead.

If you have hundreds of sets, scroll through the album first. You might find a set where you'd rather keep both copies, such as an original and a copy you saved on purpose. Tap Select on each of the other sets and leave that one alone.

What Merge keeps and where the extra copies go

Merge keeps one photo and moves the rest to Recently Deleted. Apple says the photo that stays has the highest image quality and related data from the set. The iOS 16 guide says merging combines "the highest quality version and all of the relevant data across the duplicates."

The other copies stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then they're removed for good. Until then you can get them back. Apple also has a few rules for deleted photos in general:

  • With iCloud Photos on, Apple says a photo you delete on one device is deleted on all your devices signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • If you're over your iCloud storage limit, Apple says deleted photos and videos are removed immediately and can't be recovered from Recently Deleted.
  • In a Shared Library, if someone else deletes a photo, it no longer appears for you either. Only the person who added it can recover it or permanently delete it.

Why don't I have a Duplicates album?

It means your iPhone hasn't found any duplicates yet. Apple says the album only appears after your iPhone indexes your library and finds duplicates. If it isn't there, that can also mean you don't have any.

Apple says detection needs your iPhone to be locked and connected to power. It can finish quickly or take up to a few days, depending on the size of your library and what else the phone is doing.

To see if indexing is still going, open Photos, tap Library and scroll to the bottom. If there's a status message there, indexing is still in progress. Apple doesn't say exactly how Photos decides two items are duplicates.

Sorting through near-misses yourself

Merging handles the copies Photos finds for you. Choosing between shots that are actually different, like ten tries at the same sunset where only one is sharp, is still your call. In the Photos app, tap Select, drag your finger across the ones you don't want, then tap the Trash button and confirm. To see how much space a cleanup might free, try the photo storage calculator.

If it's hard to choose from small thumbnails, Keeper shows your photos one at a time, full screen, so you swipe right to keep and left to delete. It doesn't find duplicates for you. You can start from Newest First or clear one month at a time, and nothing is deleted until you tap Save.

Common questions

Does merging duplicates delete them from iCloud?

Apple's duplicates pages don't say this directly. Merging moves the extra copies to Recently Deleted. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple says a photo you delete on one device is deleted on all your devices signed in with the same Apple Account. To keep a copy in iCloud while clearing your iPhone, see deleting from iPhone but not iCloud.

Can I get a merged duplicate back?

Open Recently Deleted under Utilities, tap View Album and unlock it with Face ID or Touch ID. Tap Select, pick the photos, tap More, then tap Recover.

Is there a free way to delete duplicate photos on iPhone?

You don't need another app. The Duplicates album is part of the Photos app in iOS 16 and later. Photos finds the duplicates on its own and puts them there for you to merge.
