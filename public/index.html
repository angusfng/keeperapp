How it works

From a full camera roll to the photos worth keeping.

Scrolling a full camera roll and tapping delete one photo at a time is how a cleanup stalls after ten minutes. Keeper turns it into a single decision per photo, made with your thumb.

  1. 1

    Pick where to start

    Shuffle, start from a photo you pick, go oldest first or newest first. Narrow it to one album when you only want to tidy part of your library.

  2. 2

    Swipe right to keep, left to delete

    Each photo fills the screen with its date, place and camera. A light tap tells you when you've crossed the keep or delete point. Swiped the wrong way? Undo slides it back.

  3. 3

    Tap Save, see the space you freed

    Keeper hands the deletions to iOS in one go, and you confirm them once. Then you see how many photos you kept, how many you cleared and how much storage came back.

Questions

Common questions

How does Keeper work?

Keeper shows the photos and videos in your library one at a time, full screen. Swipe right to keep one and left to delete it. Your choices are queued while you swipe, and nothing is removed until you tap Save. When you save, iOS asks you to confirm the deletion.

Can I get a photo back if I delete it by mistake?

Yes. While you swipe, tap undo to bring the last photo back. After you save, deleted photos go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days. Here's how to recover them.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Keeper works inside your Photos library on your iPhone or iPad through Apple's PhotosKit. It doesn't upload, send or copy your photos, and there's no account to create.

Does Keeper work with iCloud Photos?

Yes. Keeper uses your normal Photos library, so it works the same way the Photos app does. With iCloud Photos on, a photo you delete is deleted on all your devices that use iCloud Photos, and it stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days. More on that in deleting photos from iPhone but not iCloud.

Can I clean up just one album or one month?

Yes. You can limit a session to a single album, browse your library by month and clear one month at a time, or start from your oldest photos, your newest photos, a photo you pick, or a random spot.

Does Keeper find duplicate or blurry photos automatically?

No. Keeper doesn't pick photos for you. It puts each photo in front of you so you decide, which is quicker than scrolling and safer than an automatic cleaner. For exact duplicates, the Photos app has its own Duplicates album under Utilities.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. Keeper runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18 or later.

How much does Keeper cost?

Keeper is free to download and needs a weekly or yearly subscription to use. Current prices for your country are shown on the App Store and in the app. You can cancel any time in your Apple Account settings.
