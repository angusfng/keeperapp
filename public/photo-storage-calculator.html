How the calculator works

It divides the space Photos uses by the number of items to get an average size, then multiplies that by the number you'd delete. The review time is every item in your library multiplied by the seconds you'd spend on each, because you have to look at a photo to decide whether to keep it.

The average hides a lot. A minute of 4K video takes far more space than a screenshot, so deleting a few long videos can beat deleting hundreds of photos. For more ways to get space back, see how to free up iPhone storage from photos.

Common questions

How do I find out how much space my photos use?

Open Settings, tap General, then iPhone Storage. Photos is listed with the space it uses. The number of photos and videos is shown at the bottom of your Library in the Photos app.

Why is this an estimate?

Videos, Live Photos and screenshots are very different sizes, so the average size per item is only a guide. If you mostly delete videos, you'll free more than the estimate. If you mostly delete screenshots, you'll free less.

When do I get the space back?

Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted for 30 days. You can delete them from there sooner. With iCloud Photos on, deleting also removes them from iCloud and your other devices.
